Run the sprint retrospective.

1. Launch the three devs and `qa-lead` in parallel. Each one gives 2 things that went well, 2 that didn't, and
   1 suggestion, based on their journal and the sprint's tickets, bugs and decisions. They should be honest, and
   that includes about working with QA. Show their answers.
2. **Stop and ask Samin** for their own went-well, didn't-go-well and suggestion. Wait for their answer.
3. Ask `qa-lead` to reveal the seeded defects: read `team/.seeded/`, match each one to a filed bug (if any), and
   give Samin a table with Defect | Caught? | Bug | Technique that finds it. Then give a short, kind and specific
   coaching summary.
4. Ask `qa-lead` to agree 2–3 action items for the next sprint and write them into `team/decisions.md`.
