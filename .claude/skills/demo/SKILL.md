Run the sprint review.

1. Read `team/board.md`.
2. Launch the three devs in parallel. Each one gives a 3–5 line demo of their tickets that are Done (or nearly
   done): what a user can do now, and how to try it. Show their demos.
3. **Stop and ask Samin** for the quality summary: what was tested, the open bugs and their risk, the
   regression checklist result, and whether they'd ship. Wait for their answer.
4. Ask `qa-lead` for feedback on the summary and the go/no-go decision. Record the decision in `team/decisions.md`.
