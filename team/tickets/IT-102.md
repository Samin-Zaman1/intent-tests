# IT-102: `--help` prints usage and exits 0; unknown commands are named

**Type:** Story · **Owner:** Rafi (BE) · **Points:** 2 · **Reviewer:** Jonas

## Story
As a new user, I want `intent-tests --help` to show the usage and succeed, and an unknown command to tell me
which word it didn't recognize, so that I can find my way around without reading the source.

## Acceptance criteria (DRAFT: finish in `/refinement IT-102`)
- `intent-tests --help`, `-h` and `help` print the usage to stdout and exit 0
- `intent-tests frobnicate` prints `Unknown command: frobnicate` followed by the usage, and exits 2
- _Open: what does `intent-tests` with no arguments do? What about `intent-tests check --help`?_
